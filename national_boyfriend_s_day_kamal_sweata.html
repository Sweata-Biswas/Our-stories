From Sweata's Heart To Yours
To the man I want to spend rest of the life with, Sometimes I find it funny how life works. You can know someone without knowing the warmth of their hand, without having sat beside them, without knowing what their hug feels like, or what their face looks like when they’re trying not to laugh. And yet… somehow, you became someone my heart looks for. We have never stood next to each other. We have never gone on a silly little date, never fought over what to eat, never walked around somewhere with no particular destination, never sat in comfortable silence. There are so many firsts we haven't had yet. And maybe that's what makes this love so strange and beautiful to me. Because even though I haven't met you, there are parts of you that have become familiar to me. Your voice, your words, the way you talk, the little things you tell me, the conversations that somehow turn ordinary days into something I look forward to. You're miles away, and yet there are moments when you feel closer than people who are physically standing beside me. I don't know what our first meeting will be like. Maybe I'll be nervous. Maybe I'll talk too much. Maybe I'll suddenly forget every single thing I wanted to say. Maybe I'll just stare at you for a few seconds and think, "So this is the person I've been missing all this time." I wonder what it will feel like to finally see you in front of me instead of through a screen. To finally hear your voice without a phone between us. To finally be able to reach for your hand instead of wishing I could. And honestly, I think that first hug is something I'll remember for a very, very long time. Because it won't just be a hug. It'll be the moment distance finally loses. All those nights of talking. All those messages. All those moments of missing each other. All those times we've wished we could simply be together. They'll somehow lead to one moment where I finally get to look at you and say, "You're real. You're actually here." I don't want to romanticize everything and pretend distance is easy. It isn't. There are days when I wish you were here. Days when I want to tell you something and wish I could just walk over to you. Days when a message doesn't feel like enough because what I really want is your presence. I want the ordinary things with you. Not just the romantic ones. I want random grocery trips. Late-night food. Arguing about what movie to watch. Making fun of each other. Getting annoyed over stupid things and then laughing about them. Sitting together doing absolutely nothing. Taking terrible pictures. Going on spontaneous little trips. Knowing your favorite things without having to ask. Watching you fall asleep beside me. I want the kind of love where eventually, seeing you isn't a special occasion anymore. You're just… there. And somehow, that's the dream. I don't know exactly what the future has written for us. I don't want to make promises about things neither of us can control. But I do know that what I feel for you matters to me. You matter to me. And until the day distance turns into a memory, I'll hold onto the little pieces of you that I have now. Your words. Your voice. Our conversations. Our stupid jokes. Our late-night talks. The moments where we make each other smile from miles away. And maybe someday, we'll look back at all of this and laugh about how we managed to fall in love before we'd even stood in the same room. Until then, I want you to know something. I am waiting for our first hello. For our first hug. For our first walk together. For the first time I get to look at you and not through a screen. For the first ordinary day that finally belongs to both of us. And when that day comes, I hope I get to hold your hand and quietly think, "It took us this long to get here… but I'm so glad we did." Until then, take care of yourself for me. Keep smiling. Keep being you. And remember that somewhere, miles away, there is a girl who hasn't even met you yet… but already can't wait to finally meet the person her heart has been getting to know. I love you. And someday, I hope I get to tell you that while looking directly into your eyes. Happy Boyfriend's day my love ❤️❤️. Yours, Sweata ❤️
